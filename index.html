A more personal experience

Dentistry starts by listening.

Every patient is an individual. Your concerns, needs and comfort deserve to be part of the conversation.

Tripod Dental Clinic was started by three friends who wanted to provide ethical and affordable dental care in a warm, personalised setting.

The clinic describes itself as a cosy boutique practice, built around the belief that patients should be treated with the same care and consideration offered to family.

From routine dental visits to more involved treatment discussions, the aim is to help you understand your options and feel more comfortable taking the next step.

Helpful information

Questions before your visit?

A few practical details to help you choose a location and contact the clinic.

Where are Tripod Dental Clinic’s locations?

The official contact page lists three locations: Upper Boon Keng Road, Block 77 Circuit Road and Block 76 Circuit Road in Singapore.

What are the clinic’s opening hours?

The published hours are Monday to Friday, 9am–9pm, and Saturday to Sunday, 9am–5pm. The clinic lists public holidays as closed. Please confirm with your chosen branch before visiting.

What dental treatments are listed?

The services page lists general, aesthetic, restorative, surgical, orthodontic, children’s dentistry, digital radiography and dental sleep medicine services.

How do I contact the right branch?

Choose your preferred location in the clinic section above. Each branch listing includes its own telephone number and directions link.

Does Tripod Dental Clinic participate in CHAS?

The clinic’s services page identifies it as a CHAS participating clinic. Contact the clinic to confirm current eligibility and the details of any applicable subsidy for your treatment.

How can I enquire about an appointment?

Call your preferred branch using the telephone numbers listed above, or use the clinic’s official contact page to send an enquiry.
