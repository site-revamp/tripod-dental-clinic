# Tripod Dental Clinic — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Tripod Dental Clinic is a Singapore-based dental practice founded by three friends, providing general, aesthetic, restorative, orthodontic, surgical, and family dental care across three locations in Kallang and Circuit Road.

Guided by its core values of Empathy, Integrity, and Sincerity, the clinic focuses on ethical, affordable, and personalised dental treatment.

The redesigned homepage creates a modern, welcoming, and patient-focused experience that simplifies appointment enquiries, improves access to essential treatment information, and makes clinic locations easier to discover.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Appointment and Contact Journey

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage did not establish a sufficiently prominent appointment journey, requiring visitors to navigate further before identifying the appropriate clinic or contact method.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the homepage introduction with clearer patient-focused messaging and prominent appointment and contact actions. The improved navigation provides more direct pathways to selecting a clinic, contacting the team, and making appointment enquiries.

### 2. Improved Information Hierarchy and Treatment Discovery

**Previous Issue:** Important information, including treatment categories, dentist biographies, facilities, and patient benefits, was distributed across separate pages, making it harder for visitors to understand the practice's complete offering.

**Redesign Improvement:** Reorganised essential information into a more structured homepage experience, bringing dental treatments, practitioner information, facilities, and patient-care values into a clearer content hierarchy. Visitors can explore relevant services and understand the clinic's approach with less unnecessary navigation.

### 3. Stronger Three-Clinic Visibility and Accessibility

**Previous Issue:** The three clinic locations, addresses, and telephone numbers required greater prominence, particularly for patients browsing on mobile devices.

**Redesign Improvement:** Prioritised the Kallang and two Circuit Road locations through a clearer branch-information presentation, making their addresses and telephone contact options easier to identify and compare. The improved responsive layout supports more convenient clinic discovery and enquiries across devices.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Established a more logical progression from the main introduction to treatments, clinic information, and appointment actions.
- **Improved Appointment Accessibility:** Increased the prominence of contact and appointment enquiry pathways throughout the homepage.
- **Simplified Treatment Discovery:** Organised the practice's broad dental services into a more accessible and informative presentation.
- **Stronger Trust Communication:** Highlighted the clinic's patient-centred values, dental team, facilities, and approach to personalised care.
- **Improved Clinic Navigation:** Made the three branch locations and telephone details easier to discover and compare.
- **Responsive User Experience:** Prioritised readable content, convenient navigation, and accessible contact information on mobile devices.
- **Consistent Visual Presentation:** Improved spacing, typography, content organisation, and layout consistency to create a cohesive, welcoming appearance.

## Summary

The redesigned Tripod Dental Clinic homepage addresses three primary usability and communication challenges: unclear appointment pathways, fragmented treatment and practice information, and insufficient visibility of clinic locations.

The improvements establish a more organised, informative, and accessible patient journey while reinforcing the clinic's commitment to ethical, affordable, and personalised dental care.
